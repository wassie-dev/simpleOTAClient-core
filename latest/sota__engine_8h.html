Detailed Description

The update engine both clients run: boot validation, check-ins, applying an update, the trial and its confirm or rollback, and the deferred reports.

The adapter owns a sota_engine_t and calls these functions. A device runs one engine at a time (sota_engine_init()). The engine reaches the platform only through the link-time interfaces: sota_kv.h, sota_image.h, sota_boot.h and sota_sys.h from the port, sota_net.h and sota_app.h from the adapter.

Polling use: sota_engine_check(), then sota_engine_apply() when it offers an update. A managed task calls sota_engine_step() and waits the seconds it returns, waking early on sota_app_wake().

Enumerator

Flashed; the reboot is next.

SOTA_APPLY_CHECKSUM_FAIL 

The download did not match its sha256.

SOTA_APPLY_FLASH_FAIL 

Network, URL, flash or state write failure.

SOTA_APPLY_NO_OFFER 

No offer from a successful check.

SOTA_APPLY_SIGNATURE_FAIL 

Required signature missing or wrong.

SOTA_APPLY_REBOOT_PENDING 

A flashed update waits for its reboot.

SOTA_APPLY_UNCONFIRMED 

The running image is on trial; the offer is kept.

SOTA_APPLY_DOWNGRADE 

The offer's build is not above the running one.
